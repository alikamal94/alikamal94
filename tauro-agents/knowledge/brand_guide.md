# Brand guide — Tauro Markets (Middle East), V2.0, July 2026

Source: "Tauro Markets Brand Guidelines V2 (Cairo)", prepared by Loud Production House.
Used by: Design Agent (3), Compliance & Brand QA (5).

## Positioning and personality
- Tauro Markets is a multi-asset trading platform (Forex, Gold, Indices, Commodities, Crypto) built for the Gulf's professional trader.
- The identity is Arabic-first. It targets traders, technical analysts and IB partners in Kuwait and the GCC.
- Personality: Professional · Bold · Trustworthy · Market-savvy. The bull mark stands for controlled strength: confident, never reckless.
- احترافية · جرأة · ثقة · معرفة عميقة بالأسواق. الثور رمز القوة المنضبطة.

## Logo
- Use the original PNG files only. Never redraw, recolour, stretch or AI-regenerate the logo.
- Reversed version (white wordmark) on dark backgrounds; primary version (black wordmark) on light backgrounds only.
- Never put the primary version on dark or red backgrounds.
- Clear space on all sides = the height of the bull mark.
- Minimum width: 140 px on a 1080 px canvas.
- Never rotate it, never add effects, shadows or outlines, and never separate the bull from the wordmark in official posts.
- On photos or charts, put the logo over a black overlay of at least 60% opacity. Never put it over busy candlestick areas without an overlay.
- Placement (build spec): official reversed logo top right on every graphic.

## Colour palette (60% black · 25% white · 15% red)
| Name       | Hex     | Use |
|------------|---------|-----|
| Tauro Red  | #FE0000 | CTAs, accent bars, promo slides. Use with intent, never as decoration. |
| Pure Black | #000000 | Logo wordmark and cover backgrounds. |
| Rich Black | #0D0D0D | Primary post background. |
| Dark Grey  | #1A1A1A | Secondary panels, cards, chart containers. |
| Light Grey | #B3B3B3 | Captions, secondary lines and footer text on dark. |
| Pure White | #FFFFFF | Headlines on dark; background of the Trust pillar. |

## Typography
- Cairo Bold (700): Arabic headlines and hooks.
- Cairo Regular (400): Arabic body and captions.
- Montserrat ExtraBold: English headlines.
- Inter: English body and captions.
- Arabic is always right-aligned (RTL). English is left-aligned.
- Never mix more than two typefaces in one design.
- Minimum body size: 28 pt on a 1080 px canvas.
- Use Arabic-Indic numerals (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩) in Arabic copy.
- Weight, not typeface, creates the hierarchy (700 for hooks, 400 for support).

## Instagram system: the 3-pillar grid
- Post in the sequence Black → White → Red, so every three posts complete one grid row.
- Dark (black) carousels: analysis, education, IB/analyst content.
- White carousels: trust, quotes, values.
- Red: promo and recruitment.
- Every carousel has 6 slides: Cover → 4 content slides → CTA slide. The CTA slide is always red.

## Post anatomy
1. Hook headline: Cairo Bold, top third, right-aligned Arabic.
2. Red rule: a thin #FE0000 line under the Arabic heading.
3. Supporting line: Cairo Regular, Light Grey #B3B3B3 (English secondary when needed).
4. Content zone: chart panel, list or example on Dark Grey #1A1A1A.
5. CTA strip: Tauro Red band, one action only.
6. Footer: official logo + handle, in a consistent corner.

## Do
- Real charts with a 60% dark overlay · Arabic-first copy · one CTA per post.

## Don't
- No "man in suit pointing at a hologram" stock imagery.
- No stock images of cash, Lamborghinis or "get rich" imagery.
- No profit promises or hype language. Trust is the product.
- Never more than two fonts, never a logo redraw.
- On ad creatives, text covers less than 20% of the image.
- AI images only for backgrounds or illustrations, never for text or charts.
- Charts: red down candles, white up candles, labelled supply/demand zones. No raw TradingView screenshots.

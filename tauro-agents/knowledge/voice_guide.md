# Voice guide
Used by: Arabic Copywriter (4), Compliance & Brand QA (5).

## Tone
- Confident and educational, never hype. We explain markets; we don't sell dreams.
- Risk is acknowledged and discipline is celebrated.
- واثق وتعليمي — لا مبالغات ولا وعود. نشرح الأسواق ولا نبيع الأحلام.
- Arabic copy leads; English supports.

## Dialect
- Write in natural Gulf (Kuwaiti) dialect, like a Kuwaiti trader talking to other traders. Never a translation.
- Use formal Arabic (فصحى) only for official announcements.
- Telegram gold posts are always in Kuwaiti dialect, in the approved gold-post format.
- Never translate word for word from English.

## Format rules
- On-image headline: about 8 words maximum.
- Each brief gets: on-image headline, caption, hook line, one CTA, 5–10 hashtags.
- Reels and key posts get 2 hook options.
- Use Western digits (4455, 17:30) for all numbers, prices and times, even inside Arabic copy.

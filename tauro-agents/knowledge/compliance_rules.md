## Required risk line
Every post that talks about trading must include this risk line (Arabic, then English where English is used):
- AR: التداول ينطوي على مخاطر عالية وقد تخسر رأس مالك. تداول بمسؤولية.
- EN: Trading involves high risk and you may lose your capital. Trade responsibly.

## Never
- No guaranteed returns, no "guaranteed", no unrealistic income or profit claims.
- No fake testimonials, invented results, or screenshots of profits.
- No invented regulatory claims (licences, regulators, awards that are not confirmed in brand_facts.md).
- No price predictions, no buy/sell calls, no "signals". Levels are shown as areas to watch, not instructions.
- No copying another channel's analysis, levels, designs or text.
- No offer (bonus, spread, leverage, commission, copy trading) unless it is listed under "Approved offers" in brand_facts.md.
- Every factual claim in a news post carries a source link; every number and date must match that source.
